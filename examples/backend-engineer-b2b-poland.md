# Senior Backend Engineer (Go)

## Essentials

**Interview stages:** 2 stages. 1. Technical screening with the engineering lead (45min), 2. System design and team fit (90min)

**Technical assessment:** Take-home (3 hours)

**Feedback:** You hear back within 3 working days of each stage. Final decision within 5 working days of the last stage.

**Work mode:** 100% remote

**Required timezone overlap:** At least 5h overlap with UTC+1

**Contract type:** B2B

**Employment relationship:** Via staffing agency, end client: Revealed at stage 1. A Nordic fintech, roughly 200 people.

**Salary range (gross):** 150 to 200 PLN per hour

**Benefits:** Paid leave is not included in a B2B contract. Conference budget of 8000 PLN per year, hardware of your choice.

---

## Tech stack

- Required: Go, PostgreSQL, Kubernetes, experience running distributed systems in production
- Nice to have: Kafka, Terraform, previous work in a regulated financial environment

---

## What we expect from you

- Own services end to end, including what happens when they page you at night
- Review other people code with specifics rather than approvals
- Work asynchronously and write decisions down, since the team spans four countries
